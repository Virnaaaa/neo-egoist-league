Staff handbook

How the bot works

The Azure Latch bot keeps every match and its stats, rates each player out of 10 and gives them an Overall out of 110, manages the five clubs and their lineups, runs the transfer market and team money, and runs Ego Credits predictions. This guide explains each part and its commands.

The basics

Who can do what, and how to keep the bot running.

Who counts as an admin

Anyone with the Administrator or Manage Server permission. Only admins can log matches, enter stats, manage teams and run the market and Ego Credits.

Everyone else

Members can look things up: their own card, traits, leaderboards, team rosters, their Ego Credits wallet and the market. A club's Master can also submit lineups and make deals for the club.

Running the bot

Double-click Start Bot.bat on the host PC. If the bot stops, it asks R (restart) or Q (quit) and restarts by itself after 10 seconds. After any code change, the bot has to be restarted.

Where the data lives

Everything is saved in ratings.db next to the bot. Back up that one file and you have backed up the whole league.

Market test mode is on. While TEST_MODE is on (top of market.py), every /market command is admin-only and admins can act for any team. Turn it off when the league is ready to use the market itself.

/help shows a picture menu of every command. Admins also get a Staff section listing the admin commands they can use.

Matchday, step by step

The usual order for an official match. Each step lists the command and who runs it.

  1. Masters submit lineups. Each Master runs /team lineup in their club's own lineup channel: 6 starters (GK, LM, RM, LW, RW, CF), up to 6 subs, an optional opponent and the kick-off time. Only their own team sees it.
  2. Optional: open predictions. Admins create an Ego Credits fixture, markets and squad (/ec fixture, /ec market, /ec squad) so members can bet. Betting locks at kick-off.
  3. Reveal both lineups. An admin runs /matchday home away. It posts the Versus image with both lineups, pings every player and shows a kick-off countdown. Both Masters must have submitted first.
  4. Log the result. /match new with the sets, total goals, type (Scrim or Friendly) and, ideally, each set's score (e.g. 3-1 0-3 3-2 3-0). Set scores are needed for the comeback and collapse money bonuses.
  5. Enter stats. /stats add for outfield players, /stats gk for keepers, /stats extra for defensive moves, enemy-half passes and free kicks. /stats actions logs Positioning / Game IQ or imports Stat Desk totals.
  6. Record changes. Subs, position switches and style switches go in /match change, timed by set and goal. Ratings update straight away.
  7. Check it. /match view match_id shows the board: score, starting shape, every change, and each player's rating. It warns if steals and "stolen from" don't match between the teams.
  8. Automatic after that. Team money for the match is paid within seconds, players get their Ego Credits for playing, and cards and leaderboards update.
  9. Settle predictions. /ec result, then a different admin runs /ec confirm. Payouts are sent by DM.

Matches & stats

A match is first to 3 sets, and every set is first to 3 goals. Teams play 6v6, one player at each position.

🏆 Scrim

An official match. It counts for leaderboards by default, pays team money, and counts toward transfer rules.

🤝 Friendly

Unofficial. It's tracked and rated but kept separate. Switch a match's type with /match type.

Positions

GK Goalkeeper · LM / RM Left / Right Midfielder · LW / RW Left / Right Wing · CF Central Forward. The bot warns you when two teammates get the same position.

Subs and switches, in short

  • Subs happen between rounds (a new round starts after every goal). There's no limit, and a player can come back on.
  • Time is written as set + after_goal (goals already scored in that set, 0–4). 0 means before the set starts.
  • The bot refuses changes that don't add up, such as taking off someone who isn't on the field.
  • A player who switches position is rated at each position, mixed by the time spent there. The position they played longest is saved as their match position.

How ratings work

Two numbers matter: the match rating (out of 10, one per match) and the Overall (out of 110, built from all of a player's matches).

Match rating

Everyone starts at 6.0. Actions add or remove points, and the result stays between 3.0 and 10.0.

Overall and grades

Each player has 0–100 attributes built from their stats per match. Forwards get Attacking, Finishing, Passing, Dribbling and Form. Midfielders get Playmaking, Passing, Dribbling, Defending and Form. Keepers get Shot Stopping, Passing, Clean Sheets, Composure and Form. The Overall is a weighted average of the attributes × 1.1, with the key attributes for their position counting more.

S+ 100–110S 90–99A 80–89B 70–79C 60–69D 50–59E 40–49F 30–39G 0–29
  • Until a player has 3 matches, their Overall is capped at 89 and the card says PROVISIONAL.
  • Subs are judged by the time they actually played, so a short spell isn't punished.
  • The very top and bottom of each attribute are squeezed, so 90+ needs near-perfect numbers.

Opponent strength

Saved match ratings never change. Averages, though, lean on how strong the opposing squad was: a good game against a strong squad counts for more (up to ×1.2) and against a weak squad for less (down to ×0.8). This only applies once teams have 3+ scrims. /breakdown → Opponents shows the current factors.

To understand any number: /breakdown player (admins, only you see it) shows each attribute and one match rating worked out step by step from that player's own stats.

Players & cards

What players see about themselves.

/rating

The player's report card: average rating, form, positions, attribute radar, Overall and grade, stats and traits. Buttons filter by Scrims, Friendlies or one position. The 📋 button opens the skill sheet.

Card designs

Five looks: Classic, Manager Desk, Egoist, Neon HUD and Broadcast. Each player picks one from the menu under their card, and it's remembered.

/traits

33 traits in 6 categories (Attack, Passing, Dribbling, Defending, Goalkeeping, Reputation). The console shows what's earned and how close each trait is. Traits need 3+ matches.

Signature trait

A player can feature one earned trait on their card. Only that player or an admin can change it.

Styles

Every image in the styles folder is a style, named after its file. Add a PNG to add a style. /styles shows them all.

Roblox avatars

The skill sheet uses the player's full-body Roblox avatar: set it by hand with /roblox set, or it's found through Bloxlink / RoVer.

Testing without real matches

/tungtungtest (admins) gives a player fake numbers to try cards, traits and weights. Those cards are marked 🧪 TEST PROFILE. Test numbers never affect real matches, leaderboards, teams or the market. clear removes them.

/attributes [position] explains what every attribute is based on (everyone).

Teams & lineups

The five clubs are FC Barcha, PXG, Ubers, Bastard Munchen and Manshine. A player can be on one team only.

Master 👑

One per team. Submits lineups and makes transfer deals for the club. The bot doesn't hand out Discord roles; you manage roles yourself.

New Gen 🌱

One per team (the New Gen 11). New Gens can't be transferred.

Transfer market

Two separate jobs: Bidders decide what a player is worth, and Masters buy and sell players. Bidders never spend money.

Player values

  • /market value player opens a valuation round. The 4 Bidders (Discord role Bidder) each submit a price, and the round closes on its own at 4 estimates. The value is the middle of the four (the median).
  • /market valueclose closes a round early as provisional; /market valuecancel cancels or reopens it.
  • Values are frozen while the weekly window is open, so bidders work Friday to Sunday.

The transfer window

Every week from Monday 00:00 to Thursday 23:59, New York time. Deals can only happen while it's open, and unfinished deals expire when it closes.

  • /market window: open one long preseason window, pause today for a midweek matchday, or open / close it now.
  • /market channel: choose where the bot posts window news and deals.
  • /market approvals: turn admin approval of every deal on or off (on for season 1).

Four ways to move a player

DealPriceWho can stop it
/market buyThe player's valueThe selling Master accepts or refuses
/market release1.5 × value (release clause)Nobody. Needs a value set after the player had 3+ official matches
/market swapThe team getting the more valuable player pays the differenceThe other Master
/market requestThe player asks to leaveIf their Master accepts nothing, at the deadline they go to the first team that offered their value

Rules on every deal

  • New Gens can't be transferred.
  • New players are protected until they've played 4 official matches.
  • A player who just moved is locked until their new team has played 2 official matches.
  • There's no limit on squad size for now. The buyer must have the money.
  • A Master who is sold arrives at the new team as a regular player.

Admin tools: /market deals (pending deals), /market approve, /market force (complete a deal now, even outside the window).

  • /market budget [team]: a card with the balance, money in and out, a balance-history chart and the latest movements.

Ego Credits

A separate play currency (EC) for predicting matches. It can't be bought, cashed out or given away, and it has nothing to do with team money.

Earning EC

1,000 EC the first time someone opens /ec wallet (Discord account 30+ days old and a verified Roblox account). Then 60 EC per scrim played and 25 per friendly, up to 3 matches a day.

Betting limits

10–200 EC per ticket, 300 EC per person per market, 1,500 EC max return. Odds from ×1.10 to ×10. Players in the squad and admins who ran the fixture can't bet on it.

Markets

Match winner, exact set score, total goals, player X or more (goals, assists, key passes), and head-to-head between two players.

The House

Every payout comes from the House (starts at 100,000 EC). Tickets are refused if they'd risk too much of it. Admins top it up with /ec house.

Admin flow

  1. /ec fixture: the two teams and minutes until kick-off.
  2. /ec market: add markets with odds in option order (e.g. 1.70 2.10). /ec odds changes them.
  3. /ec squad: declare who plays, so they can't bet. Betting locks at kick-off, or earlier with /ec lock.
  4. Log the match and stats as usual.
  5. /ec result shows each market's outcome. Void any market whose stat wasn't really tracked (/ec void).
  6. A different admin runs /ec confirm. (The proposer can confirm alone after 12 hours.) Receipts go out by DM.

Members use /ec wallet, /ec markets, /ec tickets, /ec history and /ec richest.

Fortune cards, events & bounties

Small weekly swings in team money that keep the season interesting.

🌍 League event

Every Monday the bot picks one event for every club: a TV payout, a tax, a cheaper release-clause week, bigger sweep bonuses, a derby… It's posted in the market channel and on the website. Admins can pick it by hand with /market event.

🎴 Fortune card

Once a week the league prays for fortune (/prayforfortune) and every club draws a card at once, posted in the market channel as a newspaper page: Common (±0.5–1.5M), Rare (±2–3M or a weekly rule), Legendary (+5–6M or a strong rule) or Cursed (a painful one).

🎯 Bounties

/market bounty: the first team to beat the target's team in an official match, while holding the target scoreless, collects. A bounty stays open for 3 weeks. Masters pay from their club's budget and get it back if nobody claims it; admins put up league money.

Rule: cards and events are random, weighted to keep the league competitive.
