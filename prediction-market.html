How the odds work

Decimal odds

×2.00 means a 1 in 2 chance (50%), ×4.00 a 1 in 4 chance (25%). The shorter the odds, the bigger the favourite. These are fair odds: no house edge.

Strength

Every club starts at 1500. Each official match moves it, set by set: beating a stronger club moves it more. Once players have an Overall, a stronger squad adds to it too.

The server's picks

Each /predict pick pushes the odds toward the side it backs. The model counts as 8 picks, so a few picks nudge the odds and a crowd moves them a lot.

What you can win

The odds are a guide to who's favoured. /predict pays fixed Ego Credits whatever the odds: 30 EC for the exact score, 10 EC for the right winner.
